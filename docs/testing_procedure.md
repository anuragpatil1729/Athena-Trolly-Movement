# Testing Procedure

Never start with full-speed physical testing.

## Stage 1

Excel/path verification.

## Stage 2

Mathematical simulation.

## Stage 3

Potentiometer testing.

## Stage 4

Individual motor testing.

## Stage 5

Two-wheel drive testing at low speed.

## Stage 6

Straight-line controller.

## Stage 7

Curve controller.

## Stage 8

Node-to-node route.

## Stage 9

Full warehouse route.

A physical emergency-stop should be available during hardware tests.
